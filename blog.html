Unemployement

The great thing about blogs is they don't require that much work. Just consistency. And a way to create that isn't so demanding. Both mentally, and computationally. I use 'create' loosely. I wish I had a job. I wish I had a passion. I wish I had purpose. I know this sounds a little doomer-y but that tracks with being a leftist nowadays. It's not all bad news. But a lot of it is bad. Then again, I am nothing compared to your average software engineer. They may be getting laid off, but they're getting laid off with hundreds of thousands in savings. The rest of us... Doesn't diminish the fact that they're still worried. They get to be worried. Not just becuase being jobless can feel purposeless. But becuase there's always more. Always something you don't expect. It makes you wonder. Why are the upper class so worried?

May Day

I am a bad organizer. I allow myself to sucumb to peer pressure. The for you page alters my emotions and subsequently my decisions. And sometimes. I just don't care enough. Is that bad to say? Yes. As dozens of my comrades boycott and strike, I sit in a coffee shop mass applying for jobs. Hoping something might come up that's perfect, allows me to be financially independent, secure, and allows me to assert my autonomy. But that's what we all want. Isn't it? To have just enough. To have enough to feel like we deserve recognition for our hard work. To feel like our position in life deserves respect without having to convince people of that. Or maybe we don't all want that. Maybe I'm an anomoly. Maybe most of us wish we had something else. But I know that's not true either. Happy May Day, may the labor movement continue to push.
