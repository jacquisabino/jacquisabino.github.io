Rebranding

I knew it would happen. It always happens. I mean. What better way is there to take control of your life? To ensure people can only see the best version of you because that version of you is the most authentic. Goddammit! How could they? Choose a flaw and zoom in on it. Poke at it. Speak to it as if they have any idea where it comes from. No, no, no. That is not who we are. People must forget it exists. All of it! Forget you were once ugly. You were once broken. You were once ashamed. Forget you were once crazy. Forget you were capable of displaying emotions so strongly it left you questioning the distinction between human and animal. See, when you're new there's no such thing as an ugly version of you. Still, being viewed unfavorably, and feeling guilty does leave you with options. There is room for change. Unwanted change. But change nonetheless. You could, one, acknowledge you fucked up, live with that reality and do nothing. Absolutely not. It's not our fualt they don't know what a job is. Okay. Two, acknowledge you fucked up, and take actions to make amends to your victims. Also, irrelevant. Don't you remember who the real victims are? Right. Okay. Too expensive, and kind of dramatic. Besides, we didn't loose that much support. White supremacists make for quite a loyal fan base. Four, become a version of you that's unrecognizeable to the people you have hurt and to anyone who may have witnessed the damages. Annoying, but I've been meaning to try something fresh. Plus, you get to cover up what people clearly misunderstand. And how could they? They don't know what it's like. To be responsible. To be in charge. To make decisions that might hurt some but benefit others. To have everyone looking at you demanding "fair treatment". Like they even know what that means. They don't even know how to treat eachother fairly! They have no idea what it's like to stand for something and put your money, reputation, and marriage on the line. They don't know what it means to have people depend on you for a check. They could never know what it's like to make sacrifices. So why not rebrand? We all change right? I am frusturated. Angry. So pissed off that I think I actually could hit something right now. I'm desperate. I want all these white hippie liberals to snap out of their fucking delusions. I want everyone to stop chasing whiteness, power and control. I want people to stop pretending like they are above it all and then give me reasons that support the opposiite. You think a lifetime of pro-American, white-supremacist, pro-capitalist, anti-leftist propoganda did nothing to you? It takes more than waking up to become morally and ethically consistent. It takes more than saying you care. It takes more than a couple donations. It takes more than what you can offer in your free time. It takes radical change. A constant questioning of your choices, beliefs, spiritual practices; everything you once held onto. All of it deserves your judgement. It takes giving up the pursuit of more than you can handle. Handle equitably anyway. It takes releasing he idea that you are capable of knowing it all. Empathy is not enough. Caring is not enough. Action. Committment. Relinquishing control. That sounds easy.
