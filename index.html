Why Computer Science?

I bagan as an okay student. Nothing exceptional and nothing worth noting. But I knew how to do my school work and how to do it well. Like many smart girls, I was encouraged to go to college. Pretty much everything felt do-able except computers, engineering, and science. It felt like forbidden knowledge. I knew it wasn't common for Mexican women to take interest in this field. So I figured I'd see what's so special about it. Turns out, a lot. It's a challening world full of complex patterns and methodologies completely foreign to me. Everything felt broken. Nothing made sense. And I spent hours talking to my computer, practically begging for it to do what I wanted it to. Most days, it felt like my mind was fundamentally different from the genuises who developed Mac OS, Java, Python, and the circuitry that lies below it. And if I'm being completely honest, it still does. But when it works. It's exhilirating. There's a high. A click. The moment everything make sense. That's what Computer Science is to me. Questions and challenges looping together to create worlds. One day I hope to create something I'm truly proud of. But the world is complicated. The tech revolution has altered consciousness and the role of the computer is still to be determined, or maybe it always was. After years of schoolwork and dissapointing test scores, I admit I have a complicated relationship with Computer Science. With the industry all together. And I think that's normal. But for now, I will simply allow myself to learn from it.
