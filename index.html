About Me

Hello World, my name is Lawren Sabino but I just go by Jacqui, sorry for any confusion! I was born and raised in Memphis Tennessee. I moved to Texas in high school and have been here since. I recently graduated with a Bachelor’s in Computer Science from the University of Texas at Austin. I have a passion for technology and labor activism and am constantly looking for ways to integrate advances in technology to every day problems! Please ask and tell me your opinions on AI, information security, and privacy.

Why Computer Science?

I bagan as an okay student. Nothing exceptional and nothing worth noting. But I knew how to do my school work and how to do it well. Like many smart girls, I was encouraged to go to college. Pretty much everything felt do-able except computers. It felt like forbidden knowledge. I knew it wasn't common for Mexican women to take interest in this field. So I figured I'd see what's so special about it. Turns out, a lot. It's a challening world full of complex patterns and methodologies completely foreign to a recent high school graduate. Everything feels broken. Nothing makes sense. And I spent hours talking to my computer, practically begging for it to do what I wanted it to. Most days, it felt like my mind was fundamentally different from the genuises who developed Operating Systems, Programming Languages and the architecture propping it all up. And if I'm being completely honest, it still does. But when it works. It's exhilirating. There's a high. A click. The moment you make a connection and never go back. That's what Computer Science is to me. Questions and challenges looping together to create worlds. One day I hope to create something I'm truly proud of. But the world is complicated. The tech revolution has altered consciousness and the role of the computer is still to be determined. After years of schoolwork and dissapointing test scores, I admit I have a complicated relationship with Computer Science. With the industry all together. And I think that's normal. But for now, I continue to learn how to build, how to write, how to create, and how to share. I want the future to be full of possibility. For all of us.
